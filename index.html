Bit Planes

Dogfight biplanes and jets, earn coins, and fly with friends online.

Controls:

  • Arrows ↑↓ — thrust level.
  • Arrows ←→ — elevator controls.
  • Space — fire a gun. X — deploy a missile.
  • C — catapult / parachute and get new plane at the barn.
  • Q — drop flares to shake off missiles (F-16, A-10 and F-22).
  • M — sound on / off. Esc — menu: pause, change plane or paint, leave.

Every kill earns 10 coins. Spend them in the hangar on new planes and upgrades.
